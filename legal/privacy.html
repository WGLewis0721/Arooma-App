Privacy Policy

Arooma for iPhone · Effective 1 November 2026

Arooma keeps your fragrance collection on your iPhone. We do not collect, sell, or share your personal data.

What stays on your device

Your collection, Wishlist, wear history, saved layers, shelf names, notes, bottle photos, personal cards and app settings are stored only in Arooma's storage on your iPhone. They are included in your own iCloud or computer backups if you have device backups turned on, and they are deleted when you delete the app. There is no Arooma account and no Arooma server.

Camera and photos

Arooma uses the camera or your photo library only when you choose to capture a bottle or create a card. Reading text or a barcode from a label happens entirely on your iPhone using Apple's built-in Vision framework. Photos are not uploaded anywhere.

If you share a card image, iOS shows its standard share sheet. If you choose "Save Image", Arooma asks permission to add that one image to your photo library.

Motion

Card lighting reacts to how you tilt your iPhone using on-device motion sensors. Motion data is used only in the moment to draw the card and is never stored or sent.

No tracking, analytics or advertising

Arooma contains no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and no third-party tracking. It does not use the advertising identifier.

Purchases

This version of Arooma does not sell anything. If optional purchases are offered in the future, they will be processed by Apple through the App Store; Arooma receives only a confirmation of what you purchased, never your payment details.

Exports you create

If you export your personal cards, Arooma creates a file that you choose where to save. Arooma does not send it anywhere.

Children

Arooma does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the effective date above will change and the updated policy will be posted here. Questions: see the Arooma support page.

Arooma is an independent fragrance collection app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the fragrance houses whose products may appear in it.
