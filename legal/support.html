Contact

Report a problem or ask a question by opening an issue at github.com/WGLewis0721/Arooma-App/issues. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version.

Common questions

Where is my collection stored? Only on your iPhone, inside Arooma. It is included in your normal iCloud or computer device backup.

How do I add a bottle quickly? Tap +, type a name or take a photo, then swipe the card right for your Collection or left for your Wishlist.

The card shimmer looks off-center. Open View & Style and tap Recenter under Card lighting while holding the phone the way you normally do.

Can I turn the motion effects down? Arooma follows iOS Reduce Motion (Settings › Accessibility › Motion): card lighting holds still and the intro is static.

How do I move my personal cards to a new phone? In the Cards tab, use the menu › Export My Cards, save the file, and use Import My Cards on the new phone.

How do I delete my data? Delete the Arooma app. All data stored by Arooma is removed from the device.
